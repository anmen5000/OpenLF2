{TEN} — played with OpenLF2, with the INSANE! and XOI computer players built in
===========================================================

How to play
1. Extract the whole zip file to a folder (right-click, Extract All). Do not run it from inside the zip file.
2. Open the {TEN} folder and double-click {EXE}.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
3. Pick your fighter and add computer players (Com). On the difficulty line, cycle past CRAZY! to INSANE!, then XOI.

The computer levels
- Easy, Normal, Difficult, CRAZY!: as in the original Little Fighter 2.
- INSANE!: OpenLF2's computer player — it sees blows coming and guards, dodges and strikes back; far stronger than
  CRAZY!.
- XOI: the hardest level — INSANE! with its numbers tuned over thousands of games, and notes of training made for
  this very mod (which moves work against which fighters).

Good to know
- This folder is the mod as its author shares it (data, sprite, bg, bgm...), with OpenLF2 added: {EXE}, the .dll
  files, the scripts and licenses folders, and XOI's notes in the xoi folder.
- Play with {EXE}.
  {GOC} is the mod's original game (the old Little Fighter 2): up to CRAZY! only, without INSANE! and XOI, and it
  may give errors on a new Windows. Do not delete it or the mod's folders: {EXE} uses them.
- Keys and settings are kept in %APPDATA%\OpenLF2 (shared with OpenLF2 if it is installed on this PC).
- If the game does not open, or its window shows a single colour: update the graphics card driver.
- The game runs on OpenLF2 (https://github.com/anmen5000/OpenLF2), open source; the licences of OpenLF2 and of the
  libraries that come with it are in the licenses folder.
